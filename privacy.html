Privacy Policy

Market Compass Uploader · Last updated November 11, 2026

Who uses it

Market Compass Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of Market Compass, on the owner's own TikTok account. It does not access any other TikTok user's account or data.

What it accesses

  • The owner's basic TikTok profile (account name), to confirm which account is posting.
  • Permission to post videos to the owner's account.
  • View, like, comment and share counts for the owner's own public videos.

Where data is kept

The TikTok login tokens and the video counts are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private research files. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. No advertising or tracking is used.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.

Website

This website (earlyrotation.com) is a static site with no sign-in and no cookies set by Market Compass. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.
